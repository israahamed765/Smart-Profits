## Rules

- Merchants: any payload conflict is a hard stop. Do not pick `lastActive` at random.
- Workspaces: trusted newer JSON `savedAt` → UPDATE PostgreSQL. Do not modify JSON.
- Guards: idempotent INSERT of JSON-only UUIDs. Keep the documented **10**. Do not copy PG-only into JSON. Unknown extra PG-only is a hard stop. The two forensic `TEST_ARTIFACT` ids may be deleted from PostgreSQL only (not added to the 10).
- Events: payload identity only. Do not rewrite ids. Do not DELETE event rows.
- JSON catalog files are never deleted.
